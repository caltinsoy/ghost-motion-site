Ghost Motion Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 28, 2026

Developer: Cemalettin Altınsoy

Contact: altinsoyc@protonmail.ch

1. Overview

Ghost Motion is an iOS game. This Privacy Policy explains what information is handled when you use Ghost Motion, which third-party services may process information, and the choices available to you.

2. Information We Collect

Ghost Motion does not require you to create a custom account. We do not directly collect your name, email address, or contact information through the app.

Some features rely on services provided by third parties (Apple, Google, and RevenueCat), which may process information as described below and in their own privacy policies.

3. Game Center

Ghost Motion uses Apple Game Center for sign-in and leaderboards. When you use Game Center, Apple may process your Game Center player identity, leaderboard scores, and related Game Center information in accordance with Apple's policies. Ghost Motion submits your leaderboard scores to Game Center; it does not store your Game Center name, player ID, or photo.

4. Advertising

Ghost Motion may use Google AdMob to display interstitial ads at natural breaks between games. When ads are shown, AdMob may process device information, advertising data, approximate location derived from your IP address, app interactions, diagnostics, and your consent choices, as permitted by your region and settings.

Depending on your region, settings, and choices, ads may be non-personalized or limited. Personalized advertising is not always used.

5. Purchases

Remove Ads is a one-time App Store purchase that turns off ads in Ghost Motion. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment card details.

RevenueCat may be used to manage purchase entitlement and status (for example, whether Remove Ads is active) and to restore purchases. To do this, RevenueCat may process App Store transaction information and an anonymous app user identifier.

6. European Privacy Consent

If you are in a region where it is required (such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland), you may be shown a consent or privacy choices message before advertising data is processed. You can review or change your choices later through Privacy Choices in the app's Settings, where available.

7. Tracking

Ghost Motion does not currently request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission to access your device's advertising identifier. Third-party services described in this policy may still process other identifiers (for example, identifiers used for fraud prevention, frequency capping, purchase management, or diagnostics) as described in their own privacy policies.

8. Local Game Data

Your game progress, settings, Daily Challenge history, Endless records, and similar gameplay data are stored locally on your device unless otherwise noted in this policy (for example, scores you submit to Game Center leaderboards).

9. Children's Privacy

Ghost Motion is not specifically directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through Ghost Motion, please contact us at the address below.

10. Data Retention

Local game data remains on your device until you remove it, for example by deleting the app. Information processed by third-party services (Apple, Google, and RevenueCat) is retained according to their own retention policies.

12. Your Choices

  • Remove Ads: a one-time purchase that turns off ads in Ghost Motion.
  • Restore Purchases: restores a previous Remove Ads purchase on a new or reinstalled device.
  • Privacy Choices: where required in your region, review or change your advertising consent choices.
  • iOS and Game Center settings: you can manage Game Center sign-in and related options in the iOS Settings app.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the effective date at the top of this page.

14. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at altinsoyc@protonmail.ch.
